# P10 提示词示例与视觉校准

按需求调整主体、镜头、布局与材质，不复制项目故事。

## 视觉样本

- [环境](images/environment.png)：圆润微缩建筑、暖木色、柔和光影和局部纸艺；忽略具体房间、书本和道具。
- [角色](images/character.jpg)：平整短绒、卡通体积、衣料与细微缝线；不是其他任务的固定角色。
- [功能道具](images/prop.png)：哑光色带、软陶与断面材质分工、多视图同一对象；仅桥维修任务沿用桥块几何。

样本来自 P10 故事屋、鸭鸭和新版彩虹桥拼块，已封装，不依赖原项目绝对路径。

## 空环境底图

> Generate a 16:9 clean background of [location]. Children's picture-book 3D miniature scene, rounded matte clay-like architecture, soft hand-painted gradients, localized layered cardstock details. Bright clear [colors], gentle diffuse lighting, soft grounded shadows, readable foreground/midground/background. Keep [area] open for later character movement. Empty environment without principal characters. No labels or watermark. Avoid digital noise, sharpening halos, glossy plastic and coarse wool.

“无人”只用于纯环境需求，不复制到故事分镜。

## 角色三视图

> One consistent [character] shown in front, side and rear views at matching scale on a pale warm cream background. Follow identity reference for face, head/body ratio, costume and colors. Rounded picture-book 3D forms, fine smooth short-pile plush, soft cloth and restrained neat seams. Soft contact shadows and readable silhouettes. No captions unless requested. Avoid rough needle-felt, loose fibers, plastic skin and oversharpening.

## 功能道具编辑

> Change: match [prop] to the functional geometry in [structural reference]. Preserve: color order, thickness, material roles, model-sheet layout and background. Constraints: same object in requested views, legible mating edges and physical connections, no added motifs. Matte clay and localized papercraft with clean soft lighting.

## 分镜粗稿

> One rough storyboard sheet in [panel layout], each panel depicts [concrete shot action]. Loose confident pencil/ink lines, light construction strokes, broad marker/gouache blocks, simplified rounded plush and papercraft volumes, bright clear blues/greens/coral/pinks. Keep character identities, essential anatomy, prop geometry, screen direction and cause/effect. Shot sizes follow the screenplay. Readable blocking and framing, moderate detail; no finished glossy CGI, digital scratch noise or unwanted captions.

粗稿包含剧情角色，人物数量和格数由剧本决定。维修前后、戴帽等动作各格是清楚的具体状态，不在一格重复多份同角色姿势。
